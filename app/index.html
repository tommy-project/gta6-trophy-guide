Roadmap to the Platinum

One playthrough, no online trophies, no difficulty setting, no New Game Plus and no mission replay. The story takes about 50–60 hours; the full Platinum takes around 200, most of it on the 100% checklist after the credits.

Read this first — the only missable warning

Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy reported as possibly missable. In Chapter 1, the morning after the mission Down to Earth, follow Lucia from the kitchen to the balcony and accept her meditation. It is gone after Chapter 1, and it is not confirmed yet whether the trophy needs it — do it anyway. Also save before the Chapter 3 race against Yago (Race Against Yago): it is time-sensitive and its strand counts toward Strangers & Freaks.

Saves: the game has 15 manual save slots plus one autosave. Keep one save at the start of every chapter and one untouched save from just before the final mission. Cheats: using cheats switches off trophies and saving, so keep them off on your trophy run.

  1. Before you start. Turn on every progress and collectible notification in the settings, and Criminal Profile notifications in Settings, UI. You will want the counters visible from the first hour.
  2. Chapter 1. Play the story. Do Lucia’s balcony meditation (see the warning above). Meet Cal in Feng Shui and Kenton in Key Lento — both open long quest lines.
  3. Chapter 2. Switching between Jason and Lucia opens after Coffee Break, and most of the free-roam list opens with it. Bring your partner everywhere: meditating together counts twice. Start Billy at the Thrillbilly Mudclub, Moses for the Scooter Bros app, and the garages after Daily Driver. Scan vehicles and WAiNK spots as you travel.
  4. Chapter 3. Andy’s quests (Lucia), Tyrone’s labs (Jason), Randy’s Wish List after the Key Cloner mission, and Yasmani’s Jewelry Jobs for the Snap Gun. Do Race Against Yago the moment it appears.
  5. Chapter 4. Bank vaults open through the optional mission That’s Where The Money Is…. Rob vaults with your partner — the haul is too heavy alone, and the 4-star escape also earns Hot Together. Finish the chapter missions.
  6. Chapter 5 and the finale. Play Operation Savage Glory, then the final mission. Keep a save from just before the finale. Free roam carries on after the credits.
  7. After the credits: the 100% checklist. Open Progress, Stats, 100% Completion and work it top to bottom. The table below shows what it asks for.
  8. Clean-up. Finish the Strangers & Freaks strands, the side-character finales, and the long counts: 300 stunts, 70 of 80 challenges, 200 vehicle scans, 100 animal studies, 15 of 18 meditations, 100 WAiNK scans.
  9. Platinum. When A Long Road pops, Vice Got a Price! follows immediately.
